# Goals and funnels

## Goals

A goal marks a page or event as a successful outcome. Use page goals for stable destination paths and event goals for product actions that do not change the URL.

Examples include:

- `/signup/complete`
- `Subscription started`
- `Report exported`

Goal reports count conversions within the active date range and filters.

## Funnels

A funnel is an ordered list of page and event steps. AnalyticsHQ reports how many visitors reached each step and where they left the sequence.

Keep funnels focused. A short funnel with named product outcomes is easier to interpret than one that mirrors every screen.

## Date ranges and segments

Funnel results accept `startDate` and `endDate` and use the same filter model as the rest of AnalyticsHQ. A saved segment can narrow a funnel, subject to the minimum audience threshold described in [Filters and segments](/reports/filters-segments).
